How the Opportunity Outreach Program works.

Our objective is to reduce the burden of opportunity discovery and create a more efficient connection between procurement buyers and businesses capable of providing the required products or services.

01 · PROFILE

Understand the business

We use available business capability information and service location to understand the type of work the contractor performs and where it can provide service.

02 · MATCH

Compare against the repository

We compare that profile with contract opportunities in the BODA repository and identify opportunities whose work appears aligned.

03 · OUTREACH

Send the opportunity

When a potential match is identified, we contact the contractor by email with a complimentary contract opportunity and invite the business to participate.

04 · VISIBILITY

Provide complimentary ad space

Participating businesses receive complimentary advertising space on the BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY DEVELOPMENT AGENCY website to increase business visibility.

A potential match is an introduction, not a determination of eligibility. The contractor must review the complete solicitation, amendments, qualifications, deadlines, and other requirements from the authoritative procurement source before deciding whether to pursue the opportunity.

Designed to create value throughout the procurement process.

For Buyers

The program creates another pathway for identifying businesses whose capabilities may satisfy procurement requirements.

For Contractors

Businesses receive relevant contract opportunities based on capabilities and service locations instead of relying only on their own continuous searching.

For Participating Businesses

Businesses receive complimentary advertising space on the BODA website, providing an additional opportunity for visibility.

Complimentary participation.

There is no charge to participate in this introductory Opportunity Outreach Program or to receive the complimentary advertising space provided through the program. BODA welcomes feedback from participating businesses as the initiative develops.
