BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY DEVELOPMENT AGENCY is operated by Apropos Group LLC, an independent private company. It is not a government agency. This policy explains how information submitted through aproposopportunity.org may be collected, used, stored, and protected.

When you submit an inquiry, we may collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, business or organization name, email address, phone number if provided, state, inquiry type, message, and other information you voluntarily submit.

Website and Analytics Information

We may collect standard website-usage information such as browser or device information, referral source, pages viewed, and interaction events. Analytics services may be used to understand site performance, traffic sources, and how visitors use the website.

We may use service providers that support website hosting, data storage, communications, analytics, and related operations. Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to provide those services, comply with law, protect rights or security, or complete a business transaction involving the service. Public Procurement Information

Government contract and procurement information displayed or referenced by the site may originate from public sources. Information already publicly available is distinct from personal information submitted through an inquiry form.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy and apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted by contacting us through the website inquiry form. Requests may be subject to legal, security, recordkeeping, or operational requirements.

External Links
