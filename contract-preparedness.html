The Readiness Gap

Finding the contract is not always the final hurdle.

A business can identify a relevant contract and still face practical requirements that stand between interest and participation. A solicitation may require a bond. A jurisdiction may require a license. An agency may require specific insurance. A growing contractor may need working capital, certifications, or professional proposal support.

BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY DEVELOPMENT AGENCY is building a contract-preparedness resource network designed to make those next steps easier to identify.

Our role is to help businesses move from “I found an opportunity” toward “I understand what I may need to pursue it.”

How It Works

From opportunity to readiness.

01

Identify the requirement.

Start with the contract, solicitation, or business objective and determine the readiness requirement that needs attention.

02

Find the right resource category.

Use the preparedness network to identify the type of provider, agency, program, or professional resource that may help.

03

Connect with the provider directly.

Apropos provides the resource connection. The business evaluates the provider, services, terms, eligibility, pricing, and fit directly with that provider.

04

Move forward better prepared.

The objective is simple: reduce avoidable readiness gaps before they become barriers to bidding, award, or successful contract performance.
