Contract Technical Assistance

Government and institutional procurement can involve unfamiliar terminology, detailed requirements, registrations, certifications, insurance, bonding, deadlines, and submission rules. Technical assistance helps businesses understand what they are looking at before deciding what to do next.

Request GuidanceExplore Contract Search

Turn procurement language into practical questions.

Technical assistance is intended to help a business understand the procurement process and identify the issues that require attention. It does not replace the official solicitation or professional legal, accounting, insurance, bonding, licensing, or financial advice.

Requirement Orientation

Identify key sections of an opportunity, including scope, submission instructions, deadlines, qualifications, and requirements that need verification.

Procurement Terminology

Explain common procurement concepts such as NAICS codes, registrations, set-asides, certifications, amendments, responsiveness, and other terms encountered during opportunity review.

Readiness Questions

Help identify whether bonding, insurance, licensing, certifications, registrations, financing, proposal development, or other resources may be needed before pursuit.

Next-Step Direction

Connect the business to appropriate preparedness resources, official procurement sources, or other support when an issue requires specialized assistance.

A disciplined review sequence.

The goal is not to tell a business that it qualifies. The goal is to help the business know what must be checked.

01 · REVIEW

Understand the opportunity

Read the stated work, dates, submission method, qualifications, and major requirements.

02 · IDENTIFY

Surface the questions

Determine which requirements appear satisfied, which are uncertain, and which may require additional resources or verification.

03 · ACT

Take the appropriate next step

Verify with the official source, prepare missing requirements, seek specialized advice where needed, or decide that the opportunity is not a practical fit.

BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY DEVELOPMENT AGENCY does not determine government eligibility, issue certifications, approve bids, or guarantee awards. Businesses remain responsible for reviewing the complete solicitation and satisfying the issuing authority's requirements.

Connected support.

Technical assistance works alongside Contract Search Services and Contract Preparedness Services. The objective is a more understandable path from discovering an opportunity to deciding whether and how to pursue it.
