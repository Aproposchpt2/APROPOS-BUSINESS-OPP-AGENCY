Funding Objective

Describe what the business is trying to accomplish. Do not choose a grant, loan, or other funding product yet.

Examples: product completion, equipment, staffing, commercialization, expansion, working capital, research, or another defined business objective.

Business History

Give FDC the business context needed to understand where the company is today.

Include when the business began, what it does, important milestones, and how the current operation developed.

Current Business Need

Identify the immediate operating or development need that makes outside capital useful now.

Operations & Market Position

Capture structured business information that supports later readiness and funding-path analysis.

Only report facts the business can support. Do not estimate customers or revenue that do not exist.

Capital Requirement

Describe the business activities that need funding. FDC will develop the formal capital requirement from the business plan, costs, and evidence rather than forcing an arbitrary funding amount.

Enter structured business cost information only. Do not upload personal financial documents.

Review & Complete
