Our Purpose

Economic Growth Begins With Opportunity

Businesses are fundamental to the economic strength of a community.

They employ people. They purchase goods and services. They support suppliers. They generate commercial activity. They create pathways for entrepreneurship and advancement.

But businesses cannot pursue opportunities they cannot see. APROPOS BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY AGENCY works to improve the connection between businesses and the opportunities, markets, resources, and relationships that can help them grow.

Our purpose is not simply to distribute information. Our purpose is to help create an environment where opportunity can become economic activity.

Government Marketplace Opportunity

Public contracting can be an engine for business growth.

Every year, federal, state, county, municipal, educational, and other public institutions purchase an enormous range of products and services from businesses.

For capable businesses, participation in this marketplace can provide another pathway to revenue, experience, capacity, employment, and long-term growth.

APROPOS BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY AGENCY works to make relevant public-sector opportunities easier for businesses to discover and pursue.

When government contracts are fulfilled by qualified businesses, those businesses have an opportunity to grow, strengthen their capabilities, create jobs, engage suppliers, and contribute to the economic vitality of the communities they serve.

Local Economic Growth

Building economic capacity — city by city.

APROPOS BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY AGENCY is designed to organize its work within the communities it serves.

From there, the Agency can help identify the businesses, institutions, buyers, resources, partners, and opportunities that form the local economic ecosystem.

This creates an opportunity-development infrastructure focused on generating greater business participation and economic activity within the community.

Every local initiative begins with one question

Why Business Growth Matters

A business opportunity can reach far beyond the business.

A contract creates revenue.
Revenue can give a capable business room to invest, expand, and strengthen operations.
Revenue supports expansion.
Expansion can create new capacity, new demand, and new pathways for growth.
Expansion creates employment.
Employment can strengthen households through income, stability, and professional advancement.
Growing businesses support other businesses.
Purchasing from suppliers circulates economic activity across a broader local business ecosystem.
Successful entrepreneurs invest in community.
Communities with stronger businesses have a broader economic foundation from which to grow.
Community Impact

Businesses grow.
People prosper.
Communities become stronger.

When people gain access to meaningful employment, greater financial stability, and new opportunities for advancement, the benefits extend beyond the workplace.

Families gain greater economic security. Local purchasing power increases. Businesses gain customers. Neighborhoods gain economic activity. Communities become more resilient.

Economic development ultimately becomes meaningful when opportunity reaches people.
