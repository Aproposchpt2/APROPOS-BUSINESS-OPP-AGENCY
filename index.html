Apropos Business Opportunity Agency

Our Mission

Create access to opportunity — and convert that access into local economic growth.

Apropos Business Opportunity Agency is being established as a nonprofit public-benefit organization focused on business opportunity development. Our role is not simply to publish opportunities. Our role is to help cities build an organized pathway from local business capability to real demand.

That means identifying what businesses can provide, acquiring the opportunities and buyer demand that fit those capabilities, and helping businesses navigate the path toward participation, revenue, and sustainable growth.

Local FirstBuild around the businesses, industries, and economic priorities of each city.
Opportunity DrivenCenter services around real demand rather than generic programming.
Evidence BasedUse verified sources, procurement data, and documented market signals.
Growth MeasuredTrack participation, pursuits, buyer connections, and business outcomes.

Organized City by City

A local economic-growth program powered by a broader opportunity network.

ABOA is designed to organize where businesses live and operate: in cities and communities. Each city program can be aligned to local industries, business populations, economic-development priorities, and public-sector partners.

01

Map the Business Community

Identify the businesses operating locally, what they sell, where they are positioned to grow, and which opportunity categories matter most.

02

Build the Opportunity Feed

Bring relevant contracts, buyers, funding resources, and growth pathways into an organized opportunity environment for local businesses.

03

Measure Economic Participation

Track engagement and outcomes so cities can see how opportunity-development efforts are reaching and supporting their business community.
