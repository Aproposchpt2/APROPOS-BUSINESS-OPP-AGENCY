Contract Search Services

Finding a contract is most useful when the opportunity is relevant to the business. BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY DEVELOPMENT AGENCY helps businesses discover public-sector opportunities and examine how the stated work aligns with their capabilities.

Explore OpportunitiesRequest Guidance

Search with the business in mind.

Contract search is more than collecting solicitation links. The useful question is whether an opportunity is relevant enough to deserve a business owner's time. Search criteria can include the type of work, business capabilities, industry classification, geography, and requirements stated by the issuing authority.

Capability Alignment

Review the predominant work described by an opportunity and compare it with the services and capabilities the business actually performs.

Industry & NAICS Context

Use industry classifications and NAICS definitions as supporting context when they help clarify the work being procured.

Geographic Relevance

Consider state, local, federal, or other location requirements when geography affects whether an opportunity is practical for the business.

Source Verification

Direct businesses back to the official solicitation or authoritative procurement source for final requirements, amendments, deadlines, and submission instructions.

How the search process works.

The objective is to reduce irrelevant searching and surface opportunities that merit closer review.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Start with the business

Identify the services, capabilities, industries, and operating context that define what the business can reasonably pursue.

02 · DISCOVER

Find relevant opportunities

Search available procurement sources and identify opportunities whose work appears reasonably aligned.

03 · VERIFY

Review the official source

Confirm eligibility, scope, deadlines, certifications, bonding, licensing, insurance, and other requirements in the official solicitation.

Opportunity identification or matching does not establish eligibility and does not guarantee a contract award. The official solicitation and issuing authority control the requirements.

What happens after you find an opportunity?

A promising match may still require additional preparation. Businesses can continue to Contract Preparedness Services for readiness resources or Contract Technical Assistance for help understanding the procurement process and next-step requirements.
