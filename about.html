About Us

We believe successful businesses are the foundation of economically thriving communities.

Our Mission

To stimulate economic growth in local communities through business development.

Our Philosophy

When businesses gain access to new opportunities, acquire customers, secure contracts, and increase revenue, they create the conditions for expansion.

Business expansion creates demand for employees. Employment opportunities provide individuals and families with income, financial stability, and greater economic independence.

As household income increases, purchasing power strengthens. Consumer spending supports local businesses, generates additional economic activity, and creates opportunities for further business development.

This produces a continuing cycle of economic growth that benefits businesses, employees, families, and the communities they serve.

Our Commitment

The Business Opportunity Development Agency connects businesses with contract opportunities, business development resources, professional service providers, and tools that support customer acquisition, competitiveness, and sustainable revenue growth.

Our purpose is to help businesses turn opportunity into economic activity, creating the potential for new jobs and stronger communities.

Our Guiding Principle

Stronger Businesses. More Jobs. Stronger Communities.

ECONOMIC GROWTH THROUGH BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT.
